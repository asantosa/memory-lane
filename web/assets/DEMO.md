# Fictional photo demo

`lakeside-demo.png` was generated using the built-in image generation tool. The people and accompanying story are fictional. The app labels this content as a demo and an AI-generated photo.

Prompt: Create a single landscape 3:2 candid family snapshot with the subtle texture of a scanned 1990s color print. Two adult parents and their young child sit together at a lakeside wooden picnic table in summer, with a little rowboat and forested hills behind them. Natural imperfect candid composition, relaxed affectionate expressions, warm afternoon sunlight, ordinary casual clothes, slightly faded greens and honey tones. All people fictional. No borders, captions, text, or watermark. Intended for a nostalgic personal photo album app, legible at card size.

Demo content is bundled with the prototype and never inserted into the user's saved memories. Home offers a switch to personal memories; the library also includes the demo. Photo loading, demo hints, and story reveal were verified in Chrome Computer Use.
